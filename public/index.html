Meet Fee

Data Engineer turned Tech Founder

Hello! I'm Felisa. My career journey has taken me from sales to the intricate world of data analytics and engineering, showcasing a deep commitment to understanding and improving the technological landscapes of businesses.

Starting in sales, I developed a keen insight into customer needs and market dynamics. This foundation allowed me to transition seamlessly into sales operations, where I honed my skills in streamlining processes and enhancing productivity. My fascination with data then propelled me into roles in business intelligence and data analytics, where I could leverage data to drive strategic decisions and foster business growth.

Now, as a data and analytics engineer, I specialize in crafting robust backend solutions that support scalable and efficient data use. My work is not just about managing data; it's about empowering organizations to achieve their goals through informed, data-driven strategies.

Beyond my roles in established tech companies, I've also engaged with various side projects that allow me to explore new technologies and innovations. My most recent endeavor being PausePoint - a mindfulness scheduler designed to integrate seamlessly into daily life, enhancing personal well-being through structured and mindful scheduling.

I'm passionate about sharing my knowledge and insights, and I'm open to opportunities for speaking engagements, collaborations, and consultations. If you're looking for a speaker who can articulate complex tech concepts in an accessible and engaging way, or if you need expertise in navigating the tech landscape, I'd love to connect.
